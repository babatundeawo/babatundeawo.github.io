About

Working at the intersection of atmospheric physics, climate research, and STEM education.

Babatunde works across atmospheric physics, climate data and renewable-energy research on one side, and STEM education, curriculum design and practical technology training on the other — turning satellite datasets and scientific method into insight for researchers and into clear, hands-on lessons for learners.

His technical grounding spans solar-irradiance and wind-energy modelling, statistical analysis of climate datasets, and front-end web development, which he applies to build the very tools — dashboards, courses, and learning platforms — that he uses to teach.

Research & publications

Solar dynamics, atmospheric radiation, and wind-energy statistics.

His research combines satellite datasets, atmospheric modelling, numerical tools, and statistical validation to support renewable-energy planning across tropical regions.

M.Sc. project · Published 2020

Parametric Estimation of Direct Irradiance under All-Sky Conditions

Published in the Bulletin of the Science Association of Nigeria, the work estimated direct irradiance from publicly available satellite datasets for all-sky conditions.

  • Applied atmospheric physics to renewable-energy data challenges.
  • Supported solar planning where dense ground observations are limited.
  • Built on computational workflows using climate and satellite-data tools.
Undergraduate project · ICRERA review
