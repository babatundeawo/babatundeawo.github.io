Education journey

From Akobo classrooms to postgraduate atmospheric physics.

A learning path spanning early academic excellence, boarding-school resilience, physics, and postgraduate atmospheric science.

Boarding school experience that developed resilience, independence, and social confidence.

2009 — 2014

Completed March 2019
